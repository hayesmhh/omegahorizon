OMEGA HORIZON V9.6.9 - SOUNDTRACK IDENTITY + URGENCY
---------------------------
- Major in-game soundtrack pass built directly on V9.6.8.2; visual/gameplay systems remain protected.
- Raises stage tempos selectively to increase shmup urgency instead of applying one global speed multiplier.
- Strengthens stage-to-stage musical identity with distinct orchestral colors, ambience balance, sequencer patterns and rhythmic density.
- Adds new techno-capable procedural voices (tech pulse, acid-style sequence and synth stab) as accents beneath the established cinematic late-SNES score.
- Boosts atmospheric beds and orchestral reinforcement while preserving stereo depth and recognizable melodic material.
- Boss arrangements receive style-specific tempo lifts and stronger low ostinatos rather than merely playing the same loop faster.
- Stage 9 remains the deliberate spacious/cosmic contrast; Stage 10 becomes the strongest orchestral-techno convergence.

V9.6.9 AUDIO GATE
-----------------
