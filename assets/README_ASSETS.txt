OMEGA HORIZON AUTHORED ART ASSETS

V9.3 expands the static authored-art pipeline. These PNG files are shipped with the game and bundled into the Windows executable by PyInstaller. They are not reconstructed from runtime pygame.draw geometry.

V9.3 additions:
- title_screen_v93.png: flagship title-screen illustration
- stage01_space_v93.png: richer deep-space authored plate
- stage05_station_v93.png: rebuilt Ion Citadel interior plate
- stage08_ice_v93.png: richer frozen-moon plate
- enemy_stage01_v93.png: higher-detail Stage 1 enemy family
- enemy_stage09_v93.png: higher-detail Stage 9 enemy family
- bosses_v93.png: two-frame authored sheet for all ten bosses

Prior V9.1/V9.2 assets remain as fallbacks/history.
